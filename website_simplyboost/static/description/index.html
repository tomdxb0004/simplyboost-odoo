AI Support Agent

Put an AI support agent and product specialist on your Odoo website. It answers customers from your own documentation and product information, hands complex cases to your team, and works with the CRM and helpdesk you already use.

The agent runs on SimplyBoost, an external service: you need a SimplyBoost account with an agent set up there. This app adds that agent to the pages of your Odoo website.

What this app does in Odoo

  • Adds an AI Support Agent section to Website, Configuration, Settings.
  • Loads your agent on every page of the website you set it for. Each Odoo website can have its own agent, or none.
  • Loads nothing until you have pasted a Bot ID and agreed to send visitors' chat messages to SimplyBoost.

What the SimplyBoost agent does

Set up in your SimplyBoost account, not in Odoo.

Customer support

Answers from your manuals, FAQs, policies and website pages, in the language the customer writes in. When it cannot help or the customer asks for a person, your team is alerted with the conversation.

Product specialist

Answers questions about your products from your specifications, price lists and catalogues, compares options and asks interested visitors for their contact details with a short form.

Works with your CRM and helpdesk

Ready-made actions for HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Slack, Stripe and Google Sheets. Hand-offs can open a ticket in Zendesk, Freshdesk or Gorgias. Custom actions connect the agent to any CRM or system that has an API.

One agent, every channel

The same agent can also answer on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger, and book meetings in Google Calendar.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install this app.
  2. Copy your agent's Bot ID from the SimplyBoost dashboard (Channels, Website).
  3. In Odoo, open Website, Configuration, Settings. Under AI Support Agent, paste the Bot ID, tick Send visitors' chat messages to SimplyBoost and save.

Data sent to SimplyBoost

Nothing is sent until you set a Bot ID and tick the consent box. From then on, on the pages of that Odoo website, the chat sends SimplyBoost:

  • what visitors type in the chat;
  • the contact details they enter in the chat's contact form, if they fill it in;
  • their ratings of the agent's replies;
  • their time zone, IP address and browser details, as with any website service.

The chat keeps the conversation in the visitor's browser so it continues across pages. This app does not read or send your Odoo records (contacts, orders, invoices). SimplyBoost's privacy policy is published on simplyboost.io under Privacy Policy.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18 with the Website app.
  • A SimplyBoost account with an agent.
  • If Odoo Live Chat is also active on the website, visitors see two chat buttons; use one of them.
