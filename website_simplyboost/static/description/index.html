Your answers, your team, one chat

  • Grounded in your content. It answers from the sources you add. When it cannot find a good answer, it says so and offers your team instead of guessing.
  • Hand-over to a person. On request, or when a customer is unhappy, the conversation goes to your team with an alert, and your team replies from the SimplyBoost inbox.
  • Missing answers. Questions it could not answer are listed for you. Add the answer once and it uses it from then on.
  • One agent, every channel. The same agent can also answer on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger, with every conversation in one inbox.

Live on your Odoo website in three steps

No developer and no flow builder. Most teams have their agent answering real customers the same day.

1

Add what you know

In SimplyBoost, paste your website or sitemap, upload PDFs and Word files, or type Q&A. Each source is ready in about a minute.

2

Install this app

In Odoo, open Website, Configuration, Settings. Under AI Support Agent, paste your Bot ID (SimplyBoost dashboard: Channels, Website) and tick the consent box.

3

Save, and it is live

The chat appears on every page of that website. Each Odoo website can have its own agent, or none.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SimplyBoost AI agent for Odoo?

It is an AI chatbot for your Odoo website that handles customer support and lead generation. It answers visitors from your own content, collects their contact details and hands conversations to your team. This free app connects your SimplyBoost agent to Odoo Website.

Does it work on Odoo Online?

Yes. The app contains no Python code, so it installs on Odoo Online as well as Odoo.sh and on-premise, for Odoo 18, 19 and 20 with the Website app.

Do I need a developer?

No. You paste a Bot ID in Website settings and tick one box. There is no code and no flow to build.

What does it cost?

The Odoo app is free. The agent needs a SimplyBoost subscription: flat monthly plans of €39, €89 or €169, with no per-seat fees. A 7-day trial includes 100 AI replies.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language the customer writes in, from the same knowledge, so one agent serves all your markets.

Can it make up answers?

It answers from the sources you add. When the answer is not there, it says what it can help with, offers your team, and lists the question under Missing answers so you can fill the gap.

Does it replace Odoo Live Chat?

It can. If Odoo Live Chat stays active on the same website, visitors see two chat buttons, so most teams use one or the other.

External service and data sent to SimplyBoost

This app requires a SimplyBoost account: the agent runs on SimplyBoost's servers, hosted in Europe. Nothing is loaded or sent until you set a Bot ID and tick the consent box. From then on, on the pages of that Odoo website, the chat sends SimplyBoost:

  • what visitors type in the chat;
  • the contact details they enter in the chat's contact form, if they fill it in;
  • their ratings of the agent's replies;
  • their time zone, IP address and browser details, as with any website service.

The chat keeps the conversation in the visitor's browser so it continues across pages. This app does not read or send your Odoo records (contacts, orders, invoices). SimplyBoost's privacy policy is published on simplyboost.io under Privacy Policy.
